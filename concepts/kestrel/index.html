Questions people ask first

Can I apply on a student or graduate visa?

Yes. You need a UK address, a UK bank account and at least six months left on your visa.

Will applying hurt my score?

The eligibility check is a soft search that lenders can't see. We only do a full search if you choose to apply.

What if I miss a payment?

We'll remind you before the due date. A missed payment is reported to the credit agencies, so set up a Direct Debit to stay safe.

Can I close the card later?

Any time, in the app. Keeping an old card open usually helps your score because it shows a longer history.
