## The brief
Elmar is a Gloucester compliance consultancy run by an ISO Lead Auditor. Its Wix site was slow, hard to update and didn't reflect the quality of the work.

## How I ran it
1. **Audit:** reviewed the Wix site page by page and wrote up what was holding it back.
2. **Proposal:** a 31-page technical proposal with seven architecture diagrams, then re-scoped to fit the client's budget when the full platform was more than he needed.
3. **Build:** delivered in phases with screen-by-screen reviews with the founder, plus a snag list fixed before sign-off.
4. **Launch:** deployed on Cloudflare Pages and led the live nameserver cutover from Wix.
5. **Handover:** a CMS so the client publishes articles himself, and the repository moved into his account.

## Details that matter
- Custom SVG illustrations for each service, a navy and amber palette, and motion that respects reduced-motion settings.
- An AI chat assistant on Cloudflare Workers AI with rate limiting and no conversation storage.
- Sitemap, structured SEO and accessible forms.
