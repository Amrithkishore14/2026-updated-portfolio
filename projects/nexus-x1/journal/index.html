// THE MOBILITY TIMELINE

The Path to 2070.

A speculative look at the trajectory of intelligent mobility. Not a prediction — a vision of possibility.

2026
Electric Transition
The global shift to electric propulsion accelerates. Charging infrastructure matures. The performance gap between electric and combustion closes permanently.
2035
Connected Mobility
Vehicles communicate with infrastructure, other vehicles and their environment. The concept of driving in isolation becomes obsolete.
2050
Adaptive Transportation
Vehicles adapt in real-time to passenger needs, road conditions and energy availability. The distinction between car and service dissolves.
2070
Intelligent Mobility
The NEXUS X1 concept era. Vehicles that think, learn and predict. The road and the machine become a single system.
